Seven — Delete Your Data

This page explains how users of the Seven app can request deletion of their personal data.

How to request data deletion

Step 1. Send a data deletion request to our email address:
seven.nm.company@gmail.com
Step 2. In your request, include information that helps us identify the data associated with your use of Seven, such as your phone number or other relevant account/listing information.
Step 3. Write "Delete my Seven data" in the subject or message so that we can process your request.

What data can be deleted?

Depending on the data associated with your use of Seven, a deletion request may include:

  • Personal information provided by the user
  • Phone or contact information
  • Photos uploaded by the user
  • Listing information created by the user
  • Region or location information associated with listings
  • Technical identifiers associated with the user or device

Data that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

When data is retained for these reasons, it will be kept only for as long as necessary and handled in accordance with applicable laws and our privacy practices.

Processing your request

After receiving your request, we may contact you to verify the request and protect your data from unauthorized deletion. Once verified, we will process the deletion request.

Important: Deleting your data may permanently remove your published listings, photos, contact information, and other associated information from Seven.

Contact

If you have questions about data deletion or privacy, contact us at:
